# SqueezeBar

**Make your photos, videos, audio and PDFs smaller. Just drop them in.**

SqueezeBar sits quietly in your menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows). Drag a file onto it and get a smaller copy back in seconds, ready to email, upload or share. Everything happens on your computer: your files never leave it, and nothing is tracked.

Looking for an older version? See [Releases](../../releases).

## What it does

- **Drop and done**: drag in files and get smaller copies next to the originals.
- **Pick a size**: aim for 2, 10, 25 or 50 MB (handy for email and chat limits), choose your own, or fine-tune the quality yourself.
- **Works on almost anything**: photos (HEIC, WebP, AVIF, JPEG), videos (including GIF export), audio and PDFs.
- **Your way**: use the menu bar, a floating drop target (Mac), right-click in Finder or Explorer, the clipboard, or a watched folder that compresses new files automatically.
- **See the difference**: compare before and after, and track how much space you've saved (Mac).
